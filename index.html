INDIAN FOOD:-

Shahi Paneer

Ingredient:-

  • Paneer
  • Butter
  • Cream
  • Ginger-Garlic
  • Cashew Nuts
  • Onion and Tomatoes
  • Garam Masala
    •

    Recipe:

    First, cut the paneer into small cubes.
    Heat butter in a pan.
    Add chopped onions and tomatoes.
    Add ginger-garlic paste and cook well.
    Add cashew paste and Indian spices.
    Add fresh cream and mix well.
    Add paneer cubes and cook for 5 minutes.
    Garnish with coriander leaves.
    Serve hot with naan or roti.

    FAST FOOD:

    Pizza

    Ingredient:-

    • pizza base
    • pizza sauce
    • Mozzarella cheese
    • onion
    • capsicum
    • sweet corn
    • oregano
      •

    Pizza Recipe:

    First, take a pizza base and spread pizza sauce over it.
    Add grated mozzarella cheese on top.
    Add chopped onions, capsicum and sweet corn.
    Sprinkle oregano and chilli flakes.
    Bake the pizza until the cheese melts and the base becomes crispy.
    Finally, cut into slices and serve hot.
